Understanding Pitman 2-2-3, 4-On/4-Off, and 24/48 Shift Cycles

Standard calendar apps are designed around seven-day Monday–Friday workweeks, making multi-week rotating rosters painful to enter by hand. Emergency services, hospitals, aviation crews, semiconductor fabs, and 24/7 operations rely on deterministic cycle lengths that drift across weekdays: a 24/48 firefighter schedule repeats every 3 days (1 duty tour followed by 48 hours off), a 4-on/4-off pattern repeats every 8 days, and a Pitman 2-2-3 schedule repeats every 14 days (2 on, 2 off, 3 on, 2 off, 2 on, 3 off).

Generating a dedicated 90-day .ics roster lets you preview exact duty dates before importing, share a read-only family schedule with a spouse or co-parent, and avoid cluttering your primary personal calendar with hand-copied single events.

How to Generate and Import Your 90-Day Duty Roster

  1. Choose whether you want clean all-day duty markers (ideal for month-view readability on iPhone widgets) or timed handover shifts (such as 07:00 to 07:00 next day or 19:00 to 07:00 overnight).
  2. Enter Day 1 of your crew's confirmed cycle from your official department or employer roster—for Pitman 2-2-3, pick the first day of your 2-day work block, not the 3-day weekend block.
  3. Click Build 90-Day Shift .ICS and verify the first three generated dates in the preview against your official roster.
  4. Download shift-roster-90d.ics and import it into a dedicated color-coded calendar (for example, 'Station Duty') in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Cloud Calendars.

Handling Overnight Handovers, Kelly Days, and Shift Swaps

When a shift's end time is earlier than or equal to its start time—such as a 19:00–07:00 night shift or a 07:00–07:00 24-hour tour—the generator automatically advances DTEND to the following calendar date so busy blocks span midnight accurately.

Because each tour in the 90-day export is written as an independent VEVENT with its own UID, editing or deleting a single Kelly day, vacation day, or shift swap in your calendar app never corrupts the remaining rotation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why build a 90-day .ics file instead of an infinite RRULE?
Patterns like Pitman 2-2-3 (days 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 10, 11 of a 14-day cycle) cannot be expressed as a single weekday RRULE across all calendar clients without multiple linked series. Individual events also make swaps and Kelly days trivial to edit.
How do I share my shift calendar with my family?
Import the .ics file into a separate iCloud or Google Calendar named 'Work Shifts' and share that calendar read-only with family members.
What happens on daylight-saving change weekends?
Timed shifts exported in local station time align to wall-clock handover hours (e.g., 07:00 local). Always double-check overtime accounting with your department's payroll rules on spring-forward and fall-back nights.
