For 2027 the Queensland Government lists the Anzac Day public holiday on Monday 26 April. The Royal Queensland Show holiday on Wednesday 11 August applies to the City of Brisbane. Check the separate show holiday list for another council. The Christmas Eve entry starts at 18:00 Brisbane time. Check the business directly for branch or bank opening hours.

Import and check the calendar

Import into a separate calendar named “Brisbane holidays 2027”. The file is a snapshot of the published dates. In Apple Calendar on Mac choose File → Import. Google Calendar offers Import & export in Settings on a computer. Check the show holiday and the six-hour Christmas Eve entry after import. In a different display time zone, the Christmas Eve hours may appear at a different local time.
