Pitman 2-2-3 calendar: record a shift swap and an overtime call-back

Start with the repeating roster, then change only the affected duty. A worked overnight example keeps the next rotation intact.

Anchor the 14-day rotation

Use a confirmed first duty date. A common 2-2-3 cycle works days 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 10 and 11. A crew on the opposite rotation works the complementary days. Your published roster identifies your crew and handover time. Build a baseline in the 90-day shift generator and check both weeks against it.

Move one overnight shift

Suppose the cycle starts Monday 23 November 2026. You are scheduled Tuesday 24 November from 19:00 until Wednesday at 07:00. You agree to work Wednesday 25 November at 19:00 instead. For an imported roster with separate events, move Tuesday’s event and set its end to Thursday 26 November at 07:00. In a repeating calendar series, choose the option for this occurrence only. Your next ordinary duty still starts Friday 27 November.

Record an extra call separately

For a call-back on Thursday from 17:30 to 20:00, add a separate timed event labelled “Call-back”. That entry spans 2 hours 30 minutes. Keep the original rotation, the exchange and the additional time distinguishable. A calendar is a personal record. Use your employer’s approved timekeeping process for paid overtime.
