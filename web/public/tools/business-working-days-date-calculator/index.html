How Inclusive Business Working-Day Counts Are Calculated

Contractual notice periods, procurement SLAs, software sprint capacities, invoice net-business-day deadlines, and visa processing windows distinguish between calendar days (all 7 days of the week) and business working days (Monday through Friday). Off-by-one mistakes frequently happen when one party counts inclusively (counting both the start date and end date) and the other counts elapsed 24-hour intervals.

This calculator performs an inclusive day-by-day traversal from your Start Date through your End Date, reporting Monday–Friday working days, Saturday–Sunday weekend days, and total calendar days side by side so capacity estimates are unambiguous.

Using the Working-Days Calculator for Sprints & Deadlines

  1. Select the inclusive Start Date (for example, the first Monday of a quarter or sprint) and the inclusive End Date.
  2. Read the three figures immediately: Working Days (Monday–Friday), Weekend Days (Saturday–Sunday), and Total Calendar Days.
  3. Subtract statutory public holidays and planned team PTO days that fall on Monday–Friday in your jurisdiction—public holidays vary not only by country, but often by state, canton, or municipality.

Accounting for Regional Bank Holidays and Observed Substitutes

When a statutory holiday lands on a Saturday or Sunday, many jurisdictions (including the United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, and US federal schedules) designate the following Monday—or preceding Friday—as an observed substitute bank holiday, reducing the following week from 5 working days to 4. Always cross-check regional holiday .ics feeds when signing fixed-date delivery commitments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are both the Start Date and End Date included in the count?
Yes. Selecting Monday through Friday of the same week returns 5 working days, 0 weekend days, and 5 total calendar days.
Does this calculator automatically subtract national holidays?
No—public holidays are counted as ordinary weekdays because bank holidays differ across countries, states, and even cities (such as Brisbane's August Ekka holiday). Subtract local weekday holidays from the weekday total.
How many working days are in a typical calendar year?
A standard 365-day year contains 260 or 261 Monday–Friday weekdays before subtracting public holidays and annual leave (typically leaving ~220–235 net working days).
