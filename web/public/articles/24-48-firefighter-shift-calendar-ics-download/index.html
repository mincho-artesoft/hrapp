How to make a 24/48 firefighter shift calendar and download it as ICS

Start from one confirmed duty date, generate 90 days of 24-on/48-off shifts, and check the handover before adding the roster to your phone.

A 24/48 crew starts a tour every third calendar day. If your crew takes over on Monday, November 23, 2026 at 07:00, its next starts are Thursday, November 26 and Sunday, November 29 at the same local time. Your department’s roster determines which date belongs to your crew.

Build your crew’s 90-day calendar

  1. Open the rotating shift calendar generator. Select 24/48 Firefighter and enter the date of a confirmed duty start.
  2. For a family calendar that only needs duty dates, keep the all-day option. To show the full tour, choose local handover times and enter 07:00 for both start and end if that is your handover. The event ends at 07:00 the following day.
  3. Build the roster. Compare the first three starts with your official schedule and download the ICS file. In 90 consecutive days a regular 24/48 rotation produces 30 tours.

The example uses local station time. Import timed shifts in the station’s time zone. Around a clock change, check the displayed start and end in your destination calendar.

Get the shifts onto your iPhone

On a Mac, create a separate iCloud calendar for the roster, then use File → Import in Calendar and select that calendar. On an iPhone signed in to the same iCloud account, enable that calendar in the Calendars list. Apple’s Calendar import instructions

For Google Calendar, open Settings → Import & export on a computer, choose the .ics file and the destination calendar, then import. An import copies the roster once. A later swap in the department’s system requires an edit in your copy. Google’s import instructions

Kelly days, swaps and other rotations
