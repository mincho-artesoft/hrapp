Bring work, personal plans and weather into one calendar on iPhone and iPad. Compare selected calendars, check the forecast for your plans and keep upcoming appointments on your Home Screen.

ICLOUD, GOOGLE AND MICROSOFT IN ONE VIEW
Use iCloud calendars available on your device and connect Google Calendar. Premium adds Microsoft Calendar. Compare selected calendars while keeping their accounts and permissions clear. Choose the intended calendar when creating an event.

SHARE ONE EVENT, NOT YOUR WHOLE CALENDAR
Share an event or a calendar with email and QR invitations. Choose the access that fits the invitation. App Clip provides an event preview; adding the event requires the full app. Provider connections and sharing require sign-in where applicable.

WEATHER WHILE YOU PLAN
Compare your appointments with the hourly forecast. Check the selected place, date and available forecast before arranging an outdoor activity. Explore detailed weather views and an interactive map with wind and temperature layers. Forecast availability depends on the location and provider.

A CALENDAR VIEW FOR THE WAY YOU PLAN
Switch between Day, Week, Month, Year and List views. See work and personal commitments together, inspect all-day plans and open event details to check exact times. Use calendar views to spot overlaps before offering an apparently free slot.

SEE WHAT IS NEXT
Choose a date-and-weather layout or medium and large Home Screen widgets with upcoming event lists. Follow a countdown to your next timed appointment with Live Activity. Upcoming-event displays focus on future timed appointments; check all-day plans in the full calendar.

FROM A FORECAST HOUR TO AN EVENT DRAFT
Find a possible time for a walk, sports session or outdoor appointment in the available hourly forecast. Turn a forecast hour into an event draft, then review its time and destination calendar before saving. Weather planning and a confirmed booking are separate decisions.

CHECK A CHANGE BEFORE YOU RELY ON IT
Compare start times, durations and overlaps in the week view. Scroll day columns or open the event when you need a closer look. Before dragging or resizing an editable appointment, check its calendar, local time and repeat scope. Reopen it after saving and confirm important changes with participants.

WORK AND FAMILY PLANS, WITH CLEAR OWNERSHIP
Bring the relevant calendars into view for your weekly review. Keep a school pickup, client call or family trip in the calendar that owns it. Displaying calendars together does not merge the accounts or automatically reserve personal time in a colleague’s work calendar. A shared plan and confirmed attendance are different things.

START LOCAL, CONNECT WHEN YOU NEED TO
Create a local calendar on your device without creating an account. Local calendars are different from connected provider calendars; check the account and sharing route before relying on another device.

CHOOSE YOUR PLAN
Base is free and includes Google Calendar sync, Google Meet, calendar views, weather and widgets.
Advanced includes Base and removes ads.
Premium includes Advanced and adds Microsoft Calendar and Microsoft Teams.
