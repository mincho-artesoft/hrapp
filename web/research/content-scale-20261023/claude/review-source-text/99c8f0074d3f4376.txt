Edit your appointment schedule To adjust your availability or make other changes, you can edit your appointment schedule. Make changes to your appointment schedule On your computer, open Google Calendar . On the left, under "Booking pages," hover over the appointment schedule you want to edit. Click Options Edit . Make changes to your appointment schedule. Add availability to an appointment schedule On your computer, open Google Calendar . On the calendar, click any open time slot. Click Appointment schedule . Add a date and time. Click Add availability to an existing schedule . Select the schedule where you want to add your availability. Click Add to existing schedule . Show or hide your appointment schedules on your calendar On your computer, open Google Calendar . On the left, under "Booking pages," hover over the appointment schedule you want to show or hide. Click Options To show an appointment schedule: Click Show on calendar . To hide an appointment schedule: Click Hide from calendar . Tip: If your appointment schedule is hidden, you can: Still find booked appointments on your calendar. Create a new schedule or add time to an available one. Still allow others to book appointments with you. Learn about schedule updates, visibility & emails Learn how your appointment schedule syncs with your calendar Your appointment schedule and booked appointments automatically display on your calendar. When you select "Check calendars for availability," Google Calendar avoids schedule conflicts. If you RSVP “Yes” or “Maybe” to an event in Google Calendar, that block of time won’t display as open on your booking page. To avoid conflicts with other events, your booking page availability automatically updates. Learn how appointment schedule visibility works Anyone with edit permission on your calendar can edit the schedule and find new bookings on your calendar. Anyone with your booking page link can check for your available time slots. If you share your booking page, but not your calendar, no one can find events on your calendar. On your booking pages, if you turn off “Check calendars for availability,” all appointment time blocks show as open. However, past appointment bookings and non-appointment events accepted through booking pages remain. Even if the feature is off, others can book each appointment slot on a booking page only once. Get confirmation emails for bookings & cancellations Appointment bookings For schedules on the primary calendar: You, co-hosts, and the person who booked the appointment get an email notification. For schedules on the secondary calendar: The co-hosts and the person who booked the appointment get an email notification. Appointment cancellations For canceled appointments on the primary calendar: You, co-hosts, and the person who booked the appointment get an email notification. For canceled appointments on the secondary calendar: The co-hosts and the person who booked the appointment get an email notification. If you aren’t a co-host, set up your secondary calendar to receive email notifications for cancellations. Learn how to modify Google Calendar notifications .
