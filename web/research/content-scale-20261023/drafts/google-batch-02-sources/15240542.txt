Use Dark theme in Calendar
In Google Calendar, you can switch the default bright background to a dark background.
Turn on Dark theme in the Calendar app
Important:
You can use Dark theme in the Calendar app on Android 7 and up.
Learn how to check your Android version
.

.

Dark
.
Tips:
To change the theme of your Calendar widget and notifications,
manage your Android screen and display settings
.
For Android 10 and up:
If "battery saver mode" or "Dark theme" is enabled in your system settings, widgets and the Calendar automatically switch to a Dark theme.
For Android 9 and lower:
Widgets are always in Light theme.
