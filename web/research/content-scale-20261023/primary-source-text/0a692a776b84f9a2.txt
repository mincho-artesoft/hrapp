Fix sync problems with the Google Calendar app Important : To find your Google Calendar events in a different calendar application, like Apple Calendar or Outlook, the instructions for syncing are different: Learn how to add Calendar events to Apple Calendar Learn how to sync Calendar with computer programs Events you create in Calendar appear across all devices where you’re signed in. If you can’t find your events in the Calendar app, review your settings. First, try these common fixes Important : The Calendar app only syncs past events up to one year. To find past events beyond a year, go to calendar.google.com . Follow these steps on the device you're using the Calendar app on. 1. Check your internet connection On your mobile device, check that data or Wi-Fi is on and you’re not in airplane mode. Open a browser app and try a web search. 2. Update the Calendar app Make sure you’re using the Calendar app . In your device’s app store, update the Calendar app. 3. Check that the calendar is visible On your phone or tablet, open the Calendar app . In the top left, tap Menu . To the left of the calendar's name, make sure the box is checked. 4. Make sure new events are being added to your Google Calendar If you add an event to a calendar that’s not associated with your Google Account, it might not show up in the Calendar app or on calendar.google.com . When you create an event, check the account to which you add events. Open the Google Calendar app . In the bottom right, tap Create . At the top of the event, check that the calendar it’s added to is associated with your Google Account. Next, try these troubleshooting steps If the steps above didn’t work, read through these instructions on your computer while you try the steps on your mobile device. Check that Calendar syncs with your account Important : Some of these steps work only on Android 9.0 and up. Learn how to check your Android version . Settings can vary by phone. If the steps here don’t match your device, contact your device manufacturer . On your Android phone or tablet, open your device's Settings app. Tap Passwords, passkeys & accounts . Alternatively, tap About phone Google Account . Select your Google Account. Tap Account sync . Make sure account sync is on for “Calendar.” Confirm that the correct calendar syncs in the Calendar app Important : It might take some time for your events to show up after you turn on sync. On your Android phone or tablet, open the Google Calendar app . In the top left, tap Menu Settings . Select the calendar that doesn’t appear. If you can’t find the calendar, tap Show more . At the top of the page, next to “Sync,” make sure the setting is on. You can only find the "Sync" setting for calendars you create, but not your primary calendar. Your primary calendar displays as "Events" (unless you changed its name). Manually sync your calendar On your Android phone or tablet, open the Calendar app . At the top left, tap Menu Refresh . Make sure Calendar storage is turned on On your Android phone or tablet, open your device's Settings app. Tap Apps or Apps & notifications . To get the list of all apps, tap See all apps . In the top right, tap More Show system Calendar storage . Check your device's storage Important : When your device runs out of storage, the Calendar app stops syncing. On your Android phone or tablet, open your device's Settings app. Find the Storage section. If you need to clear space, try uninstalling apps you don't use or deleting files or photos from your device. Clear your Calendar app data Important: These steps may cause you to lose some unsynced data. Any event information you can't find on Google Calendar on your computer will be lost. Only try these steps if nothing else works. Settings can vary by phone. If the steps here don’t match your device, contact your device manufacturer . On your Android phone or tablet, open your device's Settings app. Tap the Apps See all apps . From the list of apps, tap Calendar . Tap Storage & cache Clear storage Delete . Restart your device.
