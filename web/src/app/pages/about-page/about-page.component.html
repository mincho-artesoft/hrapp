About Cloud Calendars

Published & engineered by ARTE SOFT EOOD · Sofia, Bulgaria

Independent calendar software, zero-upload RFC 5545 browser utilities, and hands-on synchronization guides tested across Apple, Google, and Microsoft ecosystems.

Our Mission

Modern schedules rarely live inside a single provider. Families coordinate on Apple iCloud, companies run on Microsoft 365 and Exchange Online, and schools, airlines, hospitals, and sports leagues publish subscriptions through Google Calendar and standard iCalendar (.ics) feeds. Cloud Calendars was built to bring those calendars—alongside hyper-local hourly weather, iPhone and iPad Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and a fast browser workspace—into one coherent view without locking users into a proprietary calendar silo.

Beyond the application itself, www.cloud-calendars.com serves as an open technical reference library and browser utility suite for anyone troubleshooting calendar synchronization, daylight-saving shifts, rotating shift rosters, or recurring event rules.

Editorial Policy & How We Test Every Guide

Calendar documentation on the web frequently goes stale when Apple, Google, or Microsoft relocate settings or change OAuth, CalDAV, and Exchange behaviors. Every guide and comparison published under Cloud Calendars Guides and Comparisons follows four editorial rules:

  • Real-Device Verification: Steps, menu paths, sync intervals, and permission prompts are verified hands-on against live Apple EventKit / iCloud CalDAV accounts, Google Calendar API v3 accounts, and Microsoft 365 / Outlook accounts on physical iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, and desktop web clients.
  • Primary Specification & Vendor Citations: Whenever a guide explains recurrence rules (RRULE, EXDATE, RDATE), time-zone identifiers (VTIMEZONE / IANA tzdb), free/busy transparency, or subscription refresh behavior, we verify claims against IETF standards (RFC 5545, RFC 5546, and RFC 7986) and official Apple, Google, and Microsoft documentation.
  • Transparent Trade-Offs: Our comparison articles document where native built-in apps or competing tools excel—such as macOS-only menu-bar workflows, team task boards, or end-to-end zero-knowledge email ecosystems—so readers can choose the right tool for their workflow.
  • Ongoing Maintenance: When iOS, Android, Google Workspace, or Outlook ship breaking UI or sync changes, affected guides are re-tested and updated with revised steps and screenshots.

Zero-Upload Browser Utilities

Our twelve free browser calendar tools—including the ICS File Viewer & Validator, ICS Merger & Deduplicator, Rotating Shift Schedule Generator, and RRULE Builder—execute 100% locally in your browser. Uploaded .ics files and schedule inputs are parsed in client-side memory and are never transmitted to our servers.

Advertising & Editorial Independence

To keep our technical troubleshooting library and browser utilities free for everyone without paywalls, public informational pages on www.cloud-calendars.com display advertising served via Google AdSense. Advertising never influences our technical recommendations or comparisons, and all authenticated calendar workspaces (/calendar/) remain strictly ad-free so private schedule data is never exposed to ad networks. For full details on cookies and privacy controls, read our Privacy Policy.
