Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 November 2026 · ARTE SOFT EOOD

How Cloud Calendars handles your calendar data, website cookies, advertising disclosures, location forecasts, and privacy rights across our website and mobile apps.

This Privacy Policy explains how ARTE SOFT EOOD ("Arte Soft", "we", "us", or "our"), based in Sofia, Bulgaria, collects, uses, and protects information when you visit www.cloud-calendars.com (the "Website", including our editorial guides, free browser utilities, and web calendar workspace) and when you use the Cloud Calendars iOS and iPadOS application (the "App", and together with the Website, the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Connected Calendar Data: When you sign in to the Web Calendar or connect third-party calendar providers (such as Apple iCloud, Google Calendar, or Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365), we process your email address, authentication tokens, and calendar event metadata strictly to display, synchronize, and manage your schedule at your direction. Your private calendar events are never sold or shared with advertising networks.
  • Zero-Upload Browser Utilities: When you use our free calendar utilities under /tools/ (such as the ICS Validator, ICS Merger, or Shift Schedule Generator), file parsing and calculations happen locally in your browser memory. Uploaded .ics files are not transmitted to or stored on our servers.
  • Location Data for Weather Forecasts: Optional weather integration uses your approximate or selected city coordinates to request hourly and multi-day weather forecasts (including from Open-Meteo). Location coordinates are not stored as a historical tracking log tied to your identity.
  • Website Usage, Log & Diagnostic Data: Like standard web services, our CDN and analytics tools (such as Google Analytics 4) process anonymized or aggregated technical data—including browser type, device category, referring pages, approximate country/region, and crash diagnostics—to keep the Services reliable and improve documentation quality.

2. Cookies, Google AdSense & Advertising Disclosures

Public informational pages, articles, comparisons, and free browser tools on www.cloud-calendars.com display advertisements through Google AdSense to fund free public access. Authenticated workspace routes (such as /calendar/), booking management routes, and legal pages are kept strictly ad-free.

  • Third-Party Advertising Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as advertising and DoubleClick cookies) and web beacons to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to www.cloud-calendars.com or other websites on the internet.
  • Personalized & Non-Personalized Ads: Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our Website and/or other sites on the internet. Visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are presented with consent choices in accordance with Google's EU User Consent Policy and the IAB TCF framework before personalized advertising cookies are set.
  • How to Opt Out of Personalized Advertising: You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices or www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • How Google Uses Partner Data: To learn more about how Google collects and processes data in connection with advertising and analytics, please review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Essential Functional Storage: Our Website also uses local browser storage (localStorage) to remember your light/dark theme preference (cc_theme) and, if you sign in to the Web Calendar, your authenticated session state.

3. How We Use Information & Legal Bases (GDPR)

For visitors and users in the EEA, UK, and other jurisdictions with data-protection laws, we process data only where we have a lawful basis:

  • Performance of a Contract: Synchronizing connected calendars, maintaining your web calendar workspace, and delivering purchased subscription features via Apple's App Store.
  • Consent: Optional location access on iOS/iPadOS, connecting third-party OAuth accounts (Google and Microsoft), and optional analytics or personalized advertising cookies where consent is required.
  • Legitimate Interests: Securing our infrastructure against abuse, fixing software defects, and serving non-intrusive contextual advertising on free public articles.
  • Legal Obligations: Complying with applicable tax, accounting, and statutory obligations in Bulgaria and the European Union.

4. Google API Services & Microsoft Graph Limited Use

Cloud Calendars' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook data is never used for serving advertisements, training generalized AI models, or building marketing profiles.

5. Data Sharing, Retention & Security

  • No Sale of Personal Data: We never sell your personal information or calendar contents.
  • Infrastructure Providers: We rely on trusted processors—including Cloudflare (edge CDN and Pages hosting), Apple (App Store billing and iCloud), Google, Microsoft, and encrypted European cloud servers—under strict confidentiality and data-protection commitments.
  • Retention: Connected account credentials and cached workspace metadata are retained only while your account remains active and are deleted upon disconnection or verified deletion request.
  • Security: All communication between your device, browser, and our servers uses TLS encryption in transit, scoped OAuth 2.0 tokens, and least-privilege access controls.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Data Deletion

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR and California privacy laws), you have the right to request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of your personal data, as well as the right to withdraw consent or revoke Google/Microsoft OAuth access at any time from your account settings. To submit a privacy or account deletion request, email privacy@cloud-calendars.com or office@arte-soft.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions requiring parental consent for information-society services), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy & Contact Information

We update this Privacy Policy whenever our features, third-party integrations, or regulatory requirements change, and publish the updated revision date at the top of this page. For any privacy questions, contact the data controller:
