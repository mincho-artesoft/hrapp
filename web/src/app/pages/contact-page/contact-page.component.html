What to Include for Faster Technical Support

If you are troubleshooting a synchronization, widget, or timezone issue, including these details helps our engineers reproduce and resolve it on the first reply (never send passwords or private calendar files unless necessary):

  • Platform & Version: iPhone, iPad, or Web browser, iOS/iPadOS version, and Cloud Calendars app version (shown at the bottom of App Settings).
  • Calendar Provider: Apple iCloud, Google Workspace / Gmail, Microsoft 365 / Exchange, or subscribed .ics URL.
  • What Happened: Whether events are missing, duplicated, shifted by a time-zone offset, or failing to refresh on a Home Screen / Lock Screen widget.
