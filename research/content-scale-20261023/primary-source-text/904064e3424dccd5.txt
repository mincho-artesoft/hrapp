Use Google Calendar in different time zones You can change your time zone and create events with certain time zones. This helps if you'll be traveling when the event happens, or if you're creating events for people in different time zones. How Calendar uses time zones When you create an event, it appears in your local time zone. It will also show up in the local time zones for anyone you invite, even if they're in a different time zone. When you travel to a different time zone, your calendar appears in the local time. Tasks adapt to the new time zone if the Calendar’s time zone is changed after a task is created. For example, a 9 AM MT task changes to an 11 AM ET task if you travel from Denver to New York. Change your time zone Important: If you are not the owner, you can't change the time zone of a calendar. Change the time zone for all your calendars On your computer, open Google Calendar . At the top right, click Settings menu Settings . On the left, under "General," click Time zone . In the "Time zone" category, click Primary time zone . Search for a city or country. Select your preferred time zone. Tip: To let Calendar confirm your primary time zone when you travel, click Ask to update my primary time zone to current location . Change the time zone of one calendar On your computer, open Google Calendar . On the left, click My calendars . Point to the calendar you want to change and click More Settings and sharing . In your calendar settings, click Time zone . Search for a city or country. Select your preferred time zone. Use other time zones Show additional time zones on your calendar grid Important: In addition to your primary time zone, you can add a secondary and a tertiary time zone. Additional time zones appear on the left of your calendar grid. On your computer, open Google Calendar . At the top right, click Settings menu Settings . On the left, under "General," click Time zone . Check the box next to Show additional time zones . Under "Secondary time zone," select your preferred time zone. (Optional) Under "Tertiary time zone," select your preferred time zone. Create an event with a different time zone On your computer, open Google Calendar . At the top left, click Create Create event More options . Next to the time for the event, click Time zone . Search for a city or country. Select your preferred time zone. If the event starts or ends in different time zones, click Use separate start and end time zones . Click OK . Fill in the details of your event click Save . Change the time zone for an event On your computer, open Google Calendar . Click the event Edit . Next to the time of the event, click Time zone . Search for a city or country. Select your preferred time zone. Click OK . Turn on a world clock On your computer, open Google Calendar . At the top right, click Settings menu Settings . In the "World clock" section, click Show world clock . Click Add time zone . Search for a city or country. Select your preferred time zone. Understand how Calendar handles daylight saving time Important: Events in the past years or future might not reflect daylight saving time changes. It might be incorrectly displayed. As the date changes within a few years, the displayed time automatically adjusts. To avoid issues with daylight saving time, Google Calendar uses Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). Learn more about UTC . When events are created, they're converted into UTC, but you'll always see them in your local time. If an area switches their time zone, events created before we knew about the change might be in the wrong time zone.
