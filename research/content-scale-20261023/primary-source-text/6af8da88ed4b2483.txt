Add a room to an event Important: To add meeting rooms and other resources to your event, you need to have a work or school account. If you have a personal Google Account that ends in @gmail.com, learn how to create a calendar for a room or shared space . You can add meeting rooms and other resources, like projectors, to your event. If you can’t book a room, learn why a Google Calendar meeting room declines an event . Google Calendar can check for room conflicts up to 12 months in advance. If you schedule events or recurring instances more than 12 months out, Google Calendar won't detect conflicts in these. Add a room to your event Important: If you can't add a room to an event, Google Calendar may suggest a shortcut to create a duplicate event where you can book a room. This shortcut creates a single event, without any guests. On your computer, open Google Calendar . Create a new event or open an existing one. To edit an existing event, click Edit event . If your account is part of an organization with Rooms, click Add rooms or Add room booking . Based on who you invite, you can choose from suggested rooms or search for a new one. Click the rooms or resources you want to add. Click Save . Tips: You can book more than one room for an event. If you add a room but its schedule is missing, in the scheduling grid, click Filter and view Show rooms . Learn how to find times to meet in Google Calendar . If you're an administrator, learn how to create rooms in your Admin Console . Remove a room from your event On your computer, open Google Calendar . Open an existing event. At the top right, click Edit event . In the "Rooms" list, find the room or resource. Next to the room or resource, click Remove room . Click Save .
