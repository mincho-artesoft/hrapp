Set your working location You can set your working location in Google Calendar so collaborators know where you are and can easily find time to meet. Calendar automatically detects the relevant time zone and suggests meeting times that match where you work. If you don't get an option to set your working location, your administrator may not have this feature enabled for your organization. Some working location features, such as specific addresses and automatic time zone updates, may not be available due to your organization's data compliance and location restrictions. Learn how to contact your administrator . Check eligibility for working location To set a working location, you need to have an eligible Google Workspace edition: Business Standard Business Plus Enterprise Starter Enterprise Standard Enterprise Plus Education Fundamentals Education Standard Education Plus Nonprofits Set your default working location Important: To set a working location, turn on working hours. Learn how to set your working hours . On your computer, open Google Calendar . At the top right, click Settings menu Settings . On the left, under "General," click Working hours & location . Select the days you work. Next to each day, enter an existing working location or create a new one. Tip: To change your working location, go to a specific day on your calendar and click the location chip. You can apply the change to that specific day or set it to repeat weekly. Add your working location On your computer, open Google Calendar . At the top right, click Create Working location . Optional: Adjust the dates that you want to add a working location to. Select a working location: Home Office Optional: To add a different location, click Other locations to search for a specific building, city, or street address. Click Save . Edit your working location for one day Important : When your calendar is shared with someone with free or busy visibility, they can get information about your availability and working location. On your computer, open Google Calendar . At the top of your calendar, click the location chip. For example, Home . At the top right, click Edit working location . Select a working location: Home Office Optional: To add a different location, click Other locations to search for a specific building, city, or street address. Click Save . Choose a frequency, and then click OK . Edit your working location for a range of days On your computer, open Google Calendar . At the top of your calendar, click the location chip. For example, Home . At the top right, click Edit working location . To adjust the date range, click on each date. Select a working location: Home Office Optional: To add a different location, click Other locations to search for a specific building, city, or street address. Click Save. Choose a frequency, and then click OK . Add your working location for part of a day On your computer, open Google Calendar . Click an empty space on your calendar. At the top of the event, click Working location . Choose the time and date you want to add a working location. Optional: To make your working location repeat: Click the day and time. Next to "Does not repeat," click the Down arrow . Choose how often you want the event to repeat and end. Choose a location or add a new one. Click Save . Tips: After you create a working location for part of your day, you can drag it to another date or time. You can set your working location to repeat. Learn how to make an existing event repeat . Delete a working location On your computer, open Google Calendar . At the top of your calendar, click the location chip. At the top right, click Delete working location . Choose the date range you want to delete for the week or your entire schedule, and then click OK . Learn how your working location is shared with others When you share your calendar with someone, they can find your working location. To help coordinate in-office time with others, your administrator may ask you to set your working location. If so, a yellow banner at the top of your calendar shows the request. If you have "Make changes and manage sharing" access to a calendar, you can edit the default working location. Learn how to choose what others can do with your calendar .
