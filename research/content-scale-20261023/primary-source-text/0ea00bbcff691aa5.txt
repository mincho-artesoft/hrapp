Change Google Calendar notifications To stay informed about your events, you can change or set up your notification settings. You get these notifications: On your phone In your web browser By email You can change your notification settings for just one event or for many events. Learn how notifications work on your account Your notification settings are personal and apply only to your account, whether you use a personal, work, or school account. No one else can change your notification settings, including people you share your calendar with or people can make changes to events on your calendar. Your specific settings don’t affect the notifications of others who have access to the calendar. Your calendar-level settings determine your notifications for new events, unless you customize them for a specific event. Learn how to turn notifications on or off You can get event notifications: By email: You get the updates in your email. As desktop notifications: When your calendar is open, these pop up outside your web browser. As alerts: They show up within your Google Calendar window. Tip: If you don’t use Calendar, you get emails when someone creates, updates, or deletes an event you’re invited to. Turn notifications on or off for all events Important: To change the settings, you need to open Calendar in a web browser, like Chrome or Safari. If you're asked to let “calendar.google.com” show notifications, you need to allow it. To change how notifications work for all your events: On your computer, open Google Calendar . At the top right, click Settings menu Settings . On the left, under “General,” click Notification settings . Under “Notification settings,” you can: Turn notifications on or off: Click the Notifications drop-down and select how you want to receive notifications. Adjust the timing of snoozed notifications: Turn on “Desktop notifications,” then click Show snoozed notifications and customize your timing. Only get notifications if you respond “Yes” or “Maybe” to an event: Click the box next to “Notify me only if I have responded Yes or Maybe.” Tip: You can get snooze notifications only on Chrome browser. If you have many notification times for the same event, the Snooze option shows only on the last one. Turn notifications on or off for one event On your computer, open Google Calendar . Click the event Edit event . Next to Notifications : To edit your notifications: Choose if you want to receive a notification or an email. You can change how often you want to get alerts in advance. To add another type of notification: Click Add notification . To remove a notification: Click Remove . At the top, click Save . Turn notifications on or off for specific calendars To change how notifications work for specific calendars you own: On your computer, open Google Calendar . At the top right, click Settings menu Settings . On the left, under “Settings for my calendars,” click the calendar you want to change Calendar settings . Under “Event notifications” and “All-day event notifications:” To edit your notifications: Choose if you want to receive a notification or an email. You can change how often you want to get alerts in advance. To add another type of notification: Click Add notification . To remove a notification: Click Remove notification . Turn email notifications on or off for specific categories To choose whether you receive email alerts for specific event changes: On your computer, open Google Calendar . At the top right, click Settings menu Settings . On the left, under "Settings for my calendars," click the name of the calendar. Click Other notifications . Next to each category, such as "Changed events" or "Event responses," click the dropdown menu. Select Email or None . Tip: If you manage multiple calendars, or receive a high volume of invitation emails, you can create custom Gmail filters to organize, label, or archive specific types of calendar updates. For example, you can search for RSVP declines with notes and archive routine accepted invitations. Learn how to filter and organize Calendar notifications in Gmail . Fix issues when Calendar notifications don't work If you don’t get event notifications, update your browser settings to allow Google Calendar to send notifications. You should also make sure that: Notifications for your calendar are on. Follow the steps above to check. Your browser can show notifications. If not, turn on desktop notifications. Google Calendar is still open in your web browser. Google Chrome On your computer, open Google Chrome . At the top right, click More Settings . Click Privacy and security Site settings Notifications . Next to “Allowed to send notifications,” click Add . In the “Site” field, enter “calendar.google.com”. Click Add . Other browsers Important : For the exact instructions, check the support site of your browser: Firefox Safari Edge On your computer, open your web browser. Go to your browser’s notification preferences. Add permissions for calendar.google.com. Tip: If you have a work or school account, your admin can make changes to events on your calendar.
