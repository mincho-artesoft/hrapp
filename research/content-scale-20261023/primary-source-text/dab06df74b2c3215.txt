Use Google Calendar in a mobile browser You can use Google Calendar in your mobile browser. Open Calendar in a mobile browser Important : If Calendar does not open in your browser, try another browser or download the Calendar app. Learn about supported browsers for Calendar . On your phone or tablet, open your browser. Go to calendar.google.com . Sign in to your Google Account. Tips : When you're offline, you can view events synced with Calendar. To create or edit events in a mobile browser, connect to the internet. After you sign in to Calendar on a mobile browser, data may be stored on your device to improve performance. From your browser’s settings, you might be able to access and clear this data. For example, you can change your website content settings on Chrome . To learn how to clear browser data, visit your browser's help center. Use Calendar in a mobile browser Important : The mobile web version of Calendar does not support notifications. To get notifications about events, install the Calendar app. Learn how to install the Calendar app . Switch between day and month view To switch between day and month view, at the top of the page, tap Day or Month . To return to the current date, tap Today . Create a new event On your phone or tablet, open a browser. Go to calendar.google.com . In the top right corner, tap Create . Add details about your event. To invite someone to your event, under "Add a person to this meeting," enter their email address. Tap Save . Edit & delete an event On your phone or tablet, open a browser. Go to calendar.google.com . Open the event you want to edit. To delete the event, tap Delete Ok . To edit the event, tap Edit . After you've made changes, tap Save . Hide or show a calendar On your phone or tablet, open a browser. Go to calendar.google.com . At the bottom right corner, tap Settings . To show or hide a calendar, tap the box next to it. Tap Save . Troubleshoot issues with Calendar in a mobile browser If you get an error: On your phone or tablet, download a different browser. In your mobile browser, turn on cookies.
