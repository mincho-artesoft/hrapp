Cancel a recurring meeting series in Outlook
Applies To
Outlook for Microsoft 365
New Outlook for Windows
Classic Outlook​​​​​​​
New Outlook
In classic Outlook, on the navigation bar, select
Calendar
.
On the calendar grid, select an instance of the meeting you want to cancel.
On the ribbon, select
Cancel Meeting
>
Cancel Series
.
Type a message to let the attendees know the future meetings are cancelled, then select
Send Cancellation
.
In new Outlook​​​​​​​, open your calendar and double-click on any meeting in the series.
Below the toolbar, select
Edit series
.
Below the end date, select the text that describes the recurrence pattern ("Occurs every ...").
In the Repeat dialog, select the end date, and change it to a date prior to the next meeting in the series. Then select
Save
.
Click
Send
to save the changes and notify the attendees.
