Update your availability for appointments You can customize your appointment schedule to make it easier for people to view your availability. Tip: Unaccepted or tentative Google Calendar events do not block appointment availability. Set appointment schedule start & end dates Important: You can only set start and end dates for recurring appointment schedules. Learn how to set a recurring appointment schedule . You can choose the dates you want your appointment schedule to start and end. On a computer, open Google Calendar . Click an existing appointment schedule Edit appointment schedule . Click Scheduling window Start and end dates . Choose the dates you want your appointment schedule to start and end. Click Done . To save the updated schedule, click Next Save . Change scheduling window and minimum and maximum notice settings By default, appointment schedules have a 4-hour minimum notice to schedule an appointment. If you check your booking page immediately after setting it up, you might not see any availability for the current day because of this minimum lead time. On a computer, open Google Calendar . Click an existing appointment schedule Edit appointment schedule . Click Scheduling window . Here you can update: Maximum time in advance that an appointment can be booked. Minimum time before the appointment starts that it can be booked. Adjust the maximum or minimum time required for someone to book an appointment with you. To save the updated schedule, click Next Save . Change a date’s availability You can choose specific dates where you’re more or less available than usual. On a computer, open Google Calendar . Click an existing appointment schedule Edit appointment schedule . Under “Adjusted availability,” click Change a date’s availability . Choose the date you want to adjust. Change the time range to show the times you’re available. If you’re not available that date at all, click Unavailable all day . To add more than one available time range, click Add a time . To remove a date exception, next to the date and time, click Remove . To save the updated schedule, click Next Save . Tip: You can adjust availability for dates that are outside of a recurring schedule with start and end dates. Manage booked appointment settings To avoid back-to-back appointments, you can turn on and adjust buffer time. On a computer, open Google Calendar . Click an existing appointment schedule Edit appointment schedule . Click Booked appointment settings . To add time between appointments: Next to “Buffer time,” check the box. Enter the buffer time. To limit the number of appointments to accept in a day: Next to “Maximum bookings per day,” check the box. Enter the maximum number of appointments to accept per day. To save the updated schedule, click Next Save .
