Print your calendar In Google Calendar, you can print your calendar for a specific date range. Customize & print your calendar On your computer, open Google Calendar . To select a date range, at the top right, click: Day Week Month Schedule 4 days At the top right, click Settings menu Print . On the "Print preview" page, change details such as font size or orientation. Click Print . Tip: When space is available, the locations or rooms of events are also displayed. Set up advanced print options Hide your calendars On the left, under "My calendars" or "Other calendars," click the name of the calendars you want to hide. If a calendar does not have a check mark next to it, it's hidden. Tip: To show a calendar, click the box. Fit your calendar on a page Change the orientation At the top right, click Settings menu Print . On the "Print preview" page, in the "Orientation" dropdown, click the Down arrow . Click Landscape or Portrait . Click Print . Hide weekends At the top right, click Settings menu Print . On the "Print preview" page, deselect Show weekends . Click Print . Change the font size At the top right, click Settings menu Print . On the "Print preview" page, in the "Font size" dropdown, click the Down arrow . Choose a font size. Click Print . Tip: If you change the font size, it won't change the number of days on a page. It may make your calendar easier to read. Save your calendar as a PDF Follow the steps to print your calendar. In the final window with print options, under "Destination," click Change . Under "Print destinations," click Save as PDF . Click Save . Tip: To find your calendar PDF check your computer’s downloads. Print your calendar in full color At the top right, click Settings menu Print . On the "Print preview" page, in the "Color & style" dropdown, click the Down arrow . Click Full color . Click Print . Tip: If “Full color” is selected, event responses appear as a: Did not respond: Transparent box No: Transparent box with crossed-out circle Yes: Full color box Maybe: Full color box with a question mark Include calendar event details At the top right, click the "View switcher" dropdown menu Schedule . At the top right, click Settings menu Print . On the "Print preview" page, select: Print descriptions Print your response Click Print .
