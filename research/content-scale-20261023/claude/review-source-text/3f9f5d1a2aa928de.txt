Teams Meeting add-in missing in new Outlook
Applies To
Outlook for Microsoft 365
Outlook 2024
Outlook 2021
Outlook 2019
Outlook 2016
The Teams meeting add-in is integrated into new Outlook. It has a
Teams meeting
toggle built into the event window, so there is no separate add‑in to install.
Teams and Outlook integration requirements
To see the
Teams meeting
toggle in new Outlook, your computer must meet the following requirements:
You must be signed in to both Outlook and Teams with the same work or school account.
You must use the new Teams client.
Reinitiate Teams integration with Outlook
If your computer meets these requirements but you don't see the
Teams meeting
toggle, use the following steps to force the Teams and Outlook integration:
Close Outlook.
Right-click the Teams icon in the toolbar and select
Quit Teams
.
Start Teams and sign in.
Start Outlook.
