Create a meeting request from email messages in new Outlook
When you create a meeting request from an email message, everyone who is on the
To
line in the original message is invited as Required Attendees, and everyone on the
Cc
line as Optional Attendees.
Do one of the following:
In the message list, select the message, and then on the
Home
tab, in the
Respond
group, select
Meeting
.
In an open message, on the
Message
tab, in the
Respond
group, select
Meeting
.
Enter the location and start and end times as you do in any meeting request. You can also add or remove attendees, use
Scheduling Assistant
to find the best meeting time, or add attachments. The header and body of the original message are included in the body of the meeting request.
Conversations and Reply with Meeting
If your message list is arranged
by conversation
, the meeting request created with the
Meeting
option is associated with the same conversation as the original message.
The meeting request appears in the expanded conversation.
When you select
Meeting
in a conversation that is not expanded and contains multiple branches, the meeting request applies to the most recent message in the selected branch.

Create a meeting request from email messages in classic Outlook
Do one of the following:
In the message list, select the message, and then on the
Home
tab, in the
Respond
group, select
Meeting
.
In an open message, on the
Message
tab, in the
Respond
group, select
Reply with Meeting.
Enter the location and start and end times as you do in any meeting request. You can also add or remove attendees, use
Scheduling Assistant
to find the best meeting time, or add attachments. The header and body of the original message are included in the body of the meeting request.
Note
The "Reply with Meeting" feature won't work for emails that are encrypted with a sensitivity label or Information Rights Management (IRM). In classic Outlook, if you try to apply the "Reply with Meeting" feature to an encrypted email, you will get an error "
You cannot perform this action. Permission to this message is restricted.⁠
"
Conversations and Reply with Meeting
If your message list is arranged
by conversation
, the meeting request created with
Meeting
is associated with the same conversation as the original message. The meeting request appears in the expanded conversation.
When you select
Meeting
on a conversation that is not expanded and contains multiple branches, the meeting request applies to the most recent message in the selected branch.
The
Meeting
command is not available when you select a conversation header.
Note
In classic Outlook client "Reply with meeting' feature is available for normal messages.
For protected messages please use "Meeting " button under 'Respond' group in 'Home' tab in New Outlook or Outlook on Web (OWA) client.
