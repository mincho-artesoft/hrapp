Let Gemini in Google Calendar find times to meet
Important:
This feature requires an eligible Google Workspace plan.
Learn about Gemini features and plans
.
To save you time, you can use Gemini in Google Calendar to search for everyone's availability and suggest times to meet that work best for everyone.
Get suggested times when you create an event
On your computer, open
Google Calendar
.
At the top left, click
Create
Event
.
Add guests to your event.
Click
Suggested times
.
Select a suggested time.
If you want to see other times, click
More suggestions
.
Or, you can directly edit on the grid.
If you edit on the grid, you can click Filter and view
. This lets you toggle rooms and filter guests by required or optional status.
Tip:
To collapse or expand the suggestions, on the left, click
Suggested times
.
Get suggested times when you reschedule an event
On your computer, open
Google Calendar
.
Click the event you want to reschedule.
Click Edit event
.
Click
Suggested times
.
Tip:
If you're the event organizer and several guests decline, a banner might appear at the top of the event with alternative schedule suggestions.
To accept the suggestion, click
Reschedule to [date and time]
.
To manually find a different time, click
More times
.
Learn why you don't get suggested times
You can't get suggested times if:
You use the Calendar app for Android, iPhone, or iPad.
The event is longer than 8 hours.
The meeting has too many attendees.
The date range you are viewing is in the past.
