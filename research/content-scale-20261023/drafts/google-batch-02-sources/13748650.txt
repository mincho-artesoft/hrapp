Add or remove alternate calendars
You can add different types of calendars, including lunar or solar, Moon phase, and sports. To add or remove alternate calendars, update your calendar settings.
There are several calendars available, like:
Lunar:
Based on the Moon’s phases.
Solar:
Based on the Sun's annual cycles.
Moon phases:
Based on the primary moon phases: New Moon, First Quarter, Full Moon, and Last Quarter.
Sports:
A schedule of upcoming sports games. You can add sports calendars for baseball, basketball, cricket, football, hockey, or rugby.
Add or remove lunar and solar calendars
On your computer, open
Google Calendar
.
At the top right, click Settings
Settings
.
Click
View options
.
Under “General,” click
Alternate calendars
drop-down.
Select your preferred calendar.
Tip:
To remove an alternate calendar, select
None
.
Add or remove Moon phases
On your computer, open
Google Calendar
.
At the top right, click Settings
Settings
.
Under “Add calendar,” click
Browse calendars of interest
.
Check or uncheck the box next to “Phases of the Moon.”
Add a Moon phase calendar
Add or remove sports calendars
On your computer, open
Google Calendar
.
At the top right, click Settings
Settings
.
Under “Add calendar,” click
Browse calendars of interest
.
Scroll to the “Sports” section.
Select a sports category.
Check or uncheck the box next to a sports team to add their game schedule to your calendar.
