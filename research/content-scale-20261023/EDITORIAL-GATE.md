# Editorial publication gate

The target is 1,100 total English articles, including the 73 recorded baseline articles. A keyword list is a planning inventory, not 1,027 approved articles. The baseline exception preserves existing pages; it is not editorial approval of their quality.

New English articles must have a record in `editorial-manifest.json`. `review.status` progresses through `candidate`, `draft`, `reviewed`, then `approved`; only approved records can correspond to a published source article. Unpublished plans can be recorded without creating routes.

Each publication record requires:

- `slug`, one unique `primaryKeyword`, and one unique `intentKey` identifying the actual reader task rather than a spelling variant;
- `readerOutcome` and `distinctValue`, describing what the reader can accomplish and what this page adds;
- `evidence`: primary sources with `url`, a claim-specific `note`, `type: "primary"`, and `verifiedAt` date. These URLs must also appear in the article's visible sources. Dates and notes must reflect an actual source check; the validator cannot verify honesty or source relevance;
- `review`: `status: "approved"`, a responsible `reviewer`, and `checkedAt` date;
- an existing related article link, substantive sections, and actionable instructions.

Exact duplicate titles, prose, keywords and intent keys fail. Substantial repeated five-word phrasing is flagged for editorial comparison and requires `overlapReview` describing the resolution. Similarity alone cannot detect all competing intent or copied ideas; an editor must compare each candidate with its closest existing page and merge when the same task is already answered. Such decisions belong in `existingOverlapSlug` and `canonicalDecision` in the planning record.

No arbitrary article word-count minimum substitutes for usefulness. Source existence, uniqueness and a passing validator do not guarantee Google indexing, ranking, firsthand testing or original insight. Never invent tests, search volumes or results.

`npm run check:editorial` audits source content against the manifest; `npm run build` also runs this gate before rendering. Topic hubs paginate English guides at 24 cards per page using ordinary crawlable links. Existing translated articles and their hubs retain their indexing hold. No new translations are generated by this infrastructure.
