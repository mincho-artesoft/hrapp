Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026 · ARTE SOFT EOOD

How Cloud Calendars handles your calendar data, your location and everything in between.

Thank you for using Cloud Calendars ("App"). This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use our App.

Information We Collect

  • Accounts and Calendar Connections: You can create local calendars without a Cloud Calendars account. Connecting Google or Microsoft calendars requires the relevant provider authorization. For Cloud Calendars account and sharing services, we store account identifiers, email addresses and names when supplied, together with ownership and access records. Provider connections use account information and authorization tokens to access the calendars you authorize.
  • Shared Content: When you share an event or calendar, its shared content is sent to and stored by Cloud Calendars' server services so it can be delivered and made available to recipients. This can include event titles, dates and times, notes, locations, recurrence details, meeting links, organizer and attendee information, calendar details, recipient addresses and access roles. Files you choose to include in shared content can also be uploaded, including their names, types and contents. These server copies are separate from local calendar storage and the original provider calendar.
  • Contacts: With the relevant Google authorization and device Contacts permission, the App can read Google contact names, email addresses and phone numbers and create or update contacts on your device. Recipient information used for invitations is also processed for sharing.
  • Usage and Advertising Data: Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform process data for advertising, ad measurement, diagnostics and consent management. Depending on the SDK and your choices, this can include device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, product interactions, crash reports and performance or diagnostic information. Some advertising data can be linked to identifiers; it is not all anonymous.
  • Device Information: Integrated advertising and consent services may process information about your device and software. When the App registers a signed-in installation for invitation notifications, our services store its installation identifier, notification token, account association and enabled or disabled status. A registration can be stored even when notification delivery is disabled; turning notifications off does not itself erase that registration.
  • Location Data (for Weather): Weather features use coordinates for a place you select or, with your device permission, your current location. Coordinates are sent to Apple WeatherKit to request weather information; weather maps also use coordinates for the area being displayed. Forecast information and selected places may be cached on your device. Locations included in shared events are part of the shared content described above.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality, including calendar synchronization and weather forecasts.
  • To process subscription payments (via Apple's App Store).
  • To respond to your support requests or inquiries.
  • To provide advertising, measure ad interactions, manage consent and diagnose performance issues through our integrated advertising services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Sharing Your Information

Information is shared to operate the services you use, deliver invitations and provide advertising as described below:

  • Service Providers and Recipients: We use cloud hosting and storage for account and sharing records, and email delivery services for invitations, including Amazon Web Services storage and Amazon SES. Shared content is made available through the sharing route and access you choose. Invitation notification services may also process routing information. Apple provides weather and App Store payment services; Google provides advertising and consent services.
  • Third-Party Services: When you connect Google Calendar or Microsoft Calendar, data is shared with those services as required by their APIs for synchronization. Refer to their respective privacy policies.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no system is completely secure.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can manage connected accounts and permissions within the App settings and your device settings.
  • You can control location access for weather features through your device settings.
  • Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Please contact us for requests. Signing out, disconnecting a provider or cancelling a subscription does not by itself erase account and sharing records held by our services or copies already received by other people.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The App uses Google Mobile Ads for advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform for consent choices. Personalized advertising is used with consent. Advertising services may use device identifiers and interactions to personalize and measure ads, subject to your consent and applicable device permissions. You can review advertising privacy choices through the consent options offered in the App and manage tracking permission in device settings. These advertising practices are separate from the account and calendar-sharing information used to provide App functionality. See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's handling of information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy within the App or by other means.
