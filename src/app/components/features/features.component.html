Your Personal Weather Command Center

Dive into a comprehensive Weather Hub for a complete meteorological overview. Experience a rich dashboard displaying the current temperature, what it truly "Feels Like", and daily highs/lows. Visualize the upcoming hourly forecast with an intuitive graph and easily scroll through the 10-day outlook. Essential metrics like Wind, Sunrise/Sunset, Moon Phase, Precipitation, Visibility, Humidity, and Pressure are presented in elegant, quick-glance summary tiles. It’s your all-in-one weather station, perfectly integrated. As seen in the screenshots, this hub is designed for clarity and depth.

Bonus Tip: Need to schedule around the weather? Tap any hour in the Weather Hub's hourly forecast to instantly create a new calendar event pre-filled for that time!

Uncover Every Nuance: Interactive Weather Detail

For those who crave meteorological depth, Cloud Calendars delivers. Tapping any module in the Weather Hub reveals stunning, interactive hourly charts. Track Temperature (actual vs. "feels like"), Precipitation (intensity & chance), UV Index (with clear risk levels), Wind (speed, gusts, direction, Beaufort scale), Humidity (and dew point), Visibility, and atmospheric Pressure. These aren't static images; they're dynamic visualizations allowing you to scrub through the day and see precise values at any given hour. Understand the full story behind the forecast, as showcased in the detailed chart screenshots.
