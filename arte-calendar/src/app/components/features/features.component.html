See Your Time, Your Way: Multiple Views

Your schedule isn't one-size-fits-all, so your calendar view shouldn't be either. Effortlessly switch perspectives to match your planning needs. Get the ultimate high-level picture with the clean **Year** view, or zoom into the **Month** view where subtle dots indicate busy days. Plan your upcoming days effectively with the detailed **Week** view, available in both portrait and convenient landscape orientations. Drill down into the nitty-gritty with the precise **Day** view's hourly grid, perfect for managing packed schedules. Or, simply scroll through your upcoming commitments with the clear, agenda-style **List** view. ChronoFlow adapts visually to how you think about and manage your time.

Fluid Event Handling: Drag & Resize

Managing your schedule should feel effortless, not cumbersome. ChronoFlow empowers you with truly intuitive event manipulation. Need to adjust an event's duration? Forget menus – simply **touch and drag the top or bottom edge** of the event block directly on the Day or Week view grid to precisely shorten or lengthen its time. Made a mistake or need to shift things around? Effortlessly **drag and drop** entire events to a new time slot within the same day, or even move them across days in the flexible Week view. It's direct manipulation that feels natural and significantly speeds up your scheduling workflow.

All Your Calendars, One Harmonious View

End the constant app-switching and bring your entire digital schedule into focus. ChronoFlow seamlessly connects with your essential calendars: **iCloud** (including Birthdays and Subscribed calendars), **Google Calendar** (available with the Advanced Plan), and **Microsoft Calendar** (Outlook/Exchange, available with the Premium Plan). The straightforward selection screens let you easily toggle individual calendars on or off, giving you granular control over what you see. Events from all your chosen sources are then intelligently merged and displayed together in your main calendar views, clearly distinguishable by their assigned **color-coding**. See your work, personal, and shared commitments all in one organized place.

Seamless Meeting Integration: Meet & Teams

Streamline your meeting workflow from creation to execution. When you create a new event within a connected Google Calendar using ChronoFlow, a **Google Meet link is automatically generated and attached** – no extra steps needed. Similarly, creating an event in a connected Microsoft Calendar instantly adds a **Microsoft Teams meeting link**. This intelligent integration saves you time and ensures your virtual meeting details are always readily available within the event. Furthermore, standard calendar invitations work seamlessly, allowing you to easily **invite participants** and keep everyone synchronized, regardless of their platform.

Contextual Weather: Plan Around the Forecast

Make weather awareness an integral part of your planning, not an afterthought. ChronoFlow cleverly embeds crucial forecast information directly within your calendar views. In the detailed **Day View**, glance to the right of the hourly grid to see clear **weather condition icons and corresponding temperatures** for each hour, letting you anticipate conditions for specific appointments. When planning further ahead using the **Week View** (in both portrait and landscape modes), a concise **daily weather summary**, including an icon and the day's projected high and low temperatures, appears conveniently at the top of each day's column. This immediate context helps you make better decisions, whether you're scheduling outdoor activities or just deciding what to wear.

Comprehensive Weather Center: At a Glance

For a complete meteorological picture, dive into the dedicated Weather tab. It presents a rich, dashboard-like experience powered by reliable forecast data. Instantly see the **current temperature**, what it actually **"Feels Like"**, and the day's expected high and low. Visualize the **hourly forecast** with an intuitive graph and condition icons. Plan further ahead with the scrollable **10-day forecast** summary. Below, quick-glance summary tiles provide essential data points: current **Wind** speed and direction, **Sunrise/Sunset** times, current **Moon Phase** (including time to next phase), total daily **Precipitation**, **Visibility**, **Humidity**, and atmospheric **Pressure**. It's your personal weather station, beautifully integrated.

Bonus Tip: Need to schedule something based on the forecast? Simply **tap on any hour** in the Weather tab's hourly forecast to instantly start creating a new calendar event pre-filled with that date and time!

Deep Dive Weather Data: Analyze the Trends

Go beyond simple icons and temperatures. For those who appreciate detail, tapping on any module in the Weather Hub reveals in-depth, **interactive hourly charts**. Visualize the expected **Precipitation** intensity (mm/h) and chance (%) throughout the day. Monitor the **UV Index** hour-by-hour, complete with clear exposure level indicators (Low to Extreme). Analyze **Wind** patterns, including sustained speed, gust potential, and cardinal/degree direction, alongside a helpful Beaufort scale reference. Track the rise and fall of **Humidity** (%) and its relation to the dew point. Observe how **Visibility** (km) is predicted to change. Follow the subtle shifts in atmospheric **Pressure** (hPa). And, of course, examine detailed **Temperature** graphs showing the forecast curve. Understand the 'why' behind the weather.
