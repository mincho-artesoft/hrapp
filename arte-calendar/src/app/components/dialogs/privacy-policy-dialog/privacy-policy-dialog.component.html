Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Cloud Calendars App

Last Updated: 05.05.2025

Thank you for using Cloud Calendars ("App"). This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use our App.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: If you create an account or connect third-party services (like Google Calendar or Microsoft Calendar via our subscription plans), we may collect information associated with those accounts as necessary to provide the service, such as your email address and calendar data access tokens. Calendar event data accessed through these services remains under the control of those services and is processed within the App only as needed to display and manage your schedule.
  • Usage Data: We may collect information about how you interact with the App, such as features used, crash reports, and performance data. This helps us improve the App. This data is often anonymized or aggregated.
  • Device Information: We may collect basic information about your device, such as device model and operating system version, primarily for troubleshooting and analytics.
  • Location Data (for Weather): The integrated weather features require access to your location (approximate or precise, depending on your settings) to provide relevant forecasts. This data is typically processed by our weather data provider and is not stored by Cloud Calendars long-term associated with your identity.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality, including calendar synchronization and weather forecasts.
  • To process subscription payments (via Apple's App Store).
  • To respond to your support requests or inquiries.
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve user experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information under limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With third-party vendors who help us operate the App (e.g., weather data providers, analytics services, cloud hosting – though calendar data is primarily processed on-device or via direct API calls). These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Third-Party Services: When you connect Google Calendar or Microsoft Calendar, data is shared with those services as required by their APIs for synchronization. Refer to their respective privacy policies.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no system is completely secure.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can manage connected accounts and permissions within the App settings and your device settings.
  • You can control location access for weather features through your device settings.
  • Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Please contact us for requests.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The App itself does not typically use cookies like websites do. However, third-party services integrated (like analytics or payment processing via the App Store) might use their own identifiers or tracking technologies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy within the App or by other means.
